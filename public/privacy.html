Privacy Policy.

Last updated: June 15, 2026

The Surge browser extension and desktop application are designed with your privacy as a foundational principle. We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any of your personal data.

All processing, downloading, and file management occurs locally on your machine. We do not integrate any analytics, telemetry, or tracking software into our products.

Your download history, preferences, connection configurations, and personal information never leave your device. They remain entirely under your control.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please feel free to open an issue on our GitHub repository.
